# Cylindrical gear reducer

## 1 Descriptions

The cylindrical gear reducer is an external meshing gear transmission device with a parallel shaft structure. It mainly has the following characteristics:

a) The gears are made of high-strength low-carbon alloy steel through carburizing and quenching, and the 
tooth surface hardness reaches HRC58~62. The gears are all ground with high precision and good 
contact.

b) High transmission efficiency: single-stage reaches 98%, double-stage reaches 96%, and three-stage 
reaches more than 95%.

c) Smooth operation and low noise.

d) Small size, light weight, long service life, high load-bearing capacity.

e) Easy installation and disassembly.

## 2 Applications

This product is widely used in metallurgy, mining, lifting, transportation, cement, construction, chemical 
industry, textile, printing and dyeing, pharmaceutical and other fields.

The applicable conditions of this series of reducers are as follows. If the following conditions are not met, 
please consult with us technically:

a) The speed of the high-speed shaft is not more than 1500 rpm.

b) The circumferential speed of the gear transmission is not more than 20 m/s.

c) The working environment temperature is -40~+45°C. When the ambient temperature is lower than 0°C,

$$
-40\sim+45^{\circ}C
$$

$$
0^{\circ}C
$$

the lubricating oil should be heated before starting (an electric heater is also provided).

d) It can run in both directions (only one-way operation is allowed when a backstop is configured).

## 8 Installation, use and maintenance

a) Installation

The axis of the input shaft and the output shaft of the reducer should be coaxial with the axis of the connecting part, and 
the error should not be greater than the allowable value of the coupling used. When the V-belt is used for input power, 
the V-belt pulley should be processed by metal cutting to reduce the unbalanced mass. It is better to use a high-strength 
narrow belt drive, which can reduce vibration noise and increase the life of the bearing.

b) Usage

After installation, the oil pool of the housing must be filled with lubricating oil, and the oil level should reach the center 
line of the oil mark.

Before the reducer is officially used, please turn the high-speed shaft by hand to ensure that it must be flexible and free 
of jamming, and then run it without load for no less than 2 hours. The operation should be smooth and free of phenomenon such as impact, vibration, noise and oil leakage. Please rectify any discovered fault.

c) Maintenance

The lubricating oil injected for the first time must be replaced after 300~600 hours of operation. The quality of the oil 
should be checked regularly during use. If the lubricating oil is mixed with impurities or aged and deteriorated oil, it 
must be replaced immediately. At the same time, check the oil level frequently. If the oil level is lower than the minimum 
oil level (center line of the oil mark), please make up for it in time. After the first oil change, if the reducer works continuously for a long time, the oil must be changed every 3,000 hours. If the reducer works no more than 8 hours a day, the 
maximum oil change time should not exceed 18 months.

During work, if the oil temperature rises significantly and exceeds 70°C, the oil temperature exceeds 100°C, the quality of 
the oil decreases, or abnormal noise occurs, please stop using and check out for causes. If the fault is caused by tooth 
surface gluing, etc., the fault must be repaired and the lubricant must be replaced before future usage.
